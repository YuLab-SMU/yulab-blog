## Research Group

The YuLab group is in the School of Basic Medical Sciences at Southern Medical University (SMU), led by Professor Yu Guangchuang. Our research direction is **bioinformatics methods and data visualization**: how to do analysis, and how to explain the analysis clearly.

A few things we mainly work on:

- **Functional enrichment and interpretation of results**: the clusterProfiler series, so that enrichment analysis results can be correctly organized, compared, and explained.
- **Phylogenetic trees and hierarchical data**: ggtree, treeio, tidytree, etc., treating trees as data objects for processing and visualization.
- **AI and scientific research workflows**: bringing Agent into real data analysis processes (for example, [Rho](https://yulab-smu.top/Rho)); what we care about is whether it can be understood and whether it can be modified further.

## This Blog

- For readers both inside and outside the group: group members can put their notes, tutorials, and project descriptions here; readers outside the group can see how we work.
- Source: the articles are in Markdown, placed in the `docs` branch of the repository, rendered into a static site with [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/), and the output is in the `gh-pages` branch.
- Updates: automatically built and published in the early morning every day; PRs are also accepted.
