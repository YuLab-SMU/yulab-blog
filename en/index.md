This is the blog of the [YuLab research group](https://yulab-smu.top/) at Southern Medical University. We write down the practices in our research, the pitfalls we have run into, and the questions we are still thinking about—not just the conclusions, but also how we got there.

What we write falls roughly into these categories:

- **Method notes**: method selection, the reasoning behind parameters, and how to interpret results.
- **Tools and projects**: how to use the R packages and tools we maintain (clusterProfiler, ggtree, treeio, ...), and why they are designed this way.
- **Group meetings and teaching**: attempts to explain something clearly—handouts, example code, reproducible analyses.
- **AI and Agent**: practices for truly integrating AI into research workflows, including whether it actually helps or gets in the way.

There is only one requirement for writing: **write for the next person who will use it**. Write the problem, data, approach, and conclusion clearly; do not leave only a result.
